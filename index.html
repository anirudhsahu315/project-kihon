SOV Particle Thrusters

Syntax Logic

Japanese follows Subject-Object-Verb syntax. Particles act as magnetic clamps specifying the function of each word block:

は
Topic Beacon (wa)
"Speaking of X..." Sets the conversational context.
が
Subject Clamp (ga)
Identifies the specific actor or highlights new information.
を
Direct Object (wo)
Marks what receives the verb's direct action.
に
Vector / Time (ni / e)
Marks destination coordinates or exact time anchors.
